# DateTimeGo Homepage Behaviors

- The digital clock updates every second from the visitor's browser time.
- The hour, minute, and second hands move from the same browser time source.
- Date and IANA time-zone labels reflect the browser locale.
- The layout is centered at desktop and mobile widths.
- The analog clock remains 200 by 200 pixels on a 390-pixel viewport.
- The calculator follows the clock in normal document flow.
- The reference clock has no click interaction; the WhatDateTime adaptation
  retains a compact 12/24-hour toggle as a useful enhancement.

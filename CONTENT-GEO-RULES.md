# Content and GEO Rules

- Answer the page query directly before supporting explanation.
- Keep calculations, dates, units, formulas, and time-zone claims deterministic and internally consistent.
- Use page-specific examples, FAQs, use cases, and internal links; do not create uniqueness through keyword substitution alone.
- Avoid unverifiable claims, invented statistics, filler, and unsupported search-volume assertions.
- Write for human comprehension and machine extraction with descriptive headings, concise answers, and valid structured data.
- Keep initial server-rendered content independent of client JavaScript and browser time zone.
